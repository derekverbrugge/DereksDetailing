Headlight restoration, careful clear-coat scratch reduction, hand washing, 3D Bead It Up ceramic spray protection, and interior surface care—all finished by hand with Chemical Guys and 3D Car Care products. No machine buffing.

For the optional $25 premium finish, I use TEC582 as a drying aid on the freshly washed, still-damp exterior: it helps the drying towel glide while laying down ceramic spray protection. After drying, I finish with 3D Bead It Up for extra gloss and slickness. 3D Bead It Up is a ceramic spray sealant, not a multi-year coating. Scratch reduction depends on clear-coat condition, and complete removal cannot be guaranteed. Services are subject to vehicle inspection.
